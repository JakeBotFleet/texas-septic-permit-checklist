# Texas Septic Permit Checklist Reference

> **Quick answer:** TCEQ OSSF permits in rural North Texas route through the county Designated Representative (DR). The seven priority counties have different routing: Palo Pinto (Public Works), Hood (Environmental Health), Erath (Environmental Health), Stephens (TCEQ finder), Young (911 Addressing), Jack (Public Works), Eastland (TCEQ finder).

## The 10-step process

1. Site evaluation
2. Soil / perc test
3. Site plan
4. Application submitted
5. Permit fee paid
6. DR review (1–3 weeks)
7. Install
8. Notify DR for inspection
9. DR inspection before backfill
10. Completion paperwork filed

## Setbacks (TCEQ §285.32 minimums)

| Feature | Setback (ft) |
|---------|--------------|
| Private well | 100 |
| Public water | 150 |
| Property line | 50 |
| Foundation | 10 |
| Surface water | 50 |
| Subsurface drain | 10 |
| Utility lines | 5 |
| Swimming pool | 15 |
